Our Story

Trained chefs.
A simple belief.

We believe eating what you love and eating healthy aren't opposites — they're the same meal, done right. Friska Wellness was built by chefs who understand that food carries emotion as much as nutrition, so every dish is designed to satisfy both.

How it started

From a kitchen in Borabanda to your table.

Friska Wellness Foods, a unit of Panthor Food Pvt Ltd, started with one frustration: healthy meal plans that tasted like punishment. Being trained chefs first, we knew food has a psychological pull — restrict it too hard and people quit. So we built the opposite: expertly balanced meals that people actually look forward to.

Tier 01

The kitchen

A dedicated cooking facility in Borabanda, Hyderabad, where every meal is prepared fresh the same day it's delivered.

Tier 02

The chefs

Internationally trained culinary team, building recipes around flavour first, nutrition and portion control built in from the start.

Tier 03

The standard

No preservatives, calorie- and portion-controlled, tuned to real goals — weight loss, high protein, or balanced everyday eating.

Tier 04

The reach

Daily delivery across Hyderabad and Secunderabad, from trial meal to full subscription.
